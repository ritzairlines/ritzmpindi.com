The method

Assess. Align. Activate.

Fast-growing companies often need senior marketing leadership before they can justify the hire. The work is strategic leadership, operational rigor and execution oversight — building programs that scale, accelerating launches, and aligning brand, growth and product teams. Seed to Series C.

Stage one

Assess

Read the position as it stands: audience, platform mix, what performs, and what the market already believes about you.

Stage two

Align

Set positioning, pillars and cadence against a defined audience, so every program works toward one outcome.

Stage three

Activate

Run the calendar, measure what moves, and keep relationships warm so the growth holds after the campaign ends.
